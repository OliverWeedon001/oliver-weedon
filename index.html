01About

Propeller & Rocket Motor Thrust Test Stand

Sheet 02.2 — Electronics & Test
Hardware + Data

I built a test rig for measuring real thrust output from different style propellers using a small motor, a load cell and an Arduino to control everything. The base version tests brushless propeller motors; a second variant was adapted to test small rocket motors, logging thrust over the full burn, however I haven't tested it yet due to extreme weather and the risk of starting wild fires.

Role: Mechanical design, electronics, firmware, data analysis Tools: Arduino, load cell + amplifier, brushless motor/ESC, 3D-printed jig Status: Complete, two variants

The stand mounts the motor rigidly against a load cell, with the Arduino sampling force in real time and streaming it out for logging.
