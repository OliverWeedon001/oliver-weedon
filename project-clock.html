3D-Printed Mechanical Clock

A fully working mechanical clock, designed from scratch in CAD and manufactured entirely on a home 3D printer — every gear, escapement part, and housing component modelled before it was ever printed.

aOverview

The goal was to see how far a fully mechanical timekeeping device could be taken using nothing but a home FDM printer — no off-the-shelf clock movement, no bought gears. Every part that moves, from the mainspring drum down to the escapement wheel, was modelled from first principles.
